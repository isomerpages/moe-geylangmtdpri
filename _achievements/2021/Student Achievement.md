<h4>SYF Art Exhibition</h4>
<p>Our students' artworks in both P3/4 and P5/6 categories were selected
for display at this year's SYF Art Exhibition. The artworks will be conferred
either the Certificate of Recognition or Certificate of Recognition (Special
Mention).</p>
